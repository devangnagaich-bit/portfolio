# AfterHrs Launch Campaign

Campaign graphics for **AfterHrs**, a streetwear brand (Instagram: @afterhrs.in), built around its first drop, Drop 01 Hoodies.

## Look and feel

Black, torn paper, taped photos and cracked concrete textures, with bold condensed type in bone white and a few touches of red. Brand line: "Streetwear for night owls."

## The set

- Coming-soon poster: "AfterHrs, First Drop, Coming soon. Drop 01, Streetwear", in two versions
- Sneak-peek posts and stories, with product frames ready for photos and "Swipe up to be notified"
- Countdown story ("03 days to go") and a "3 days to go" feed post
- "First drop this Friday" announcement post
- "Drop 01 Hoodies" product post template
- Brand posts: "After hours, AfterHrs", "Streetwear for night owls" and "Follow us for the first drop"
- A scrapbook-style collage template

The full set is in my Google Drive as AfterHrs.pdf.
