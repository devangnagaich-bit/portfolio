# DOSO NGO Digital Assets

DOSO NGO is a children's education, health and care organisation based in Lucknow, India. I co-founded it with Alka Singh (Director) and Paridhi Singh (Creative Head), and I handle marketing and AI.

Core programmes: school outreach, child sponsorship and sponsor transparency reporting.

## What I built

- **Website:** a 15-page multi-site with interactive presentations, personal sites for team members, certificates, posters, brochures and social media content.
- **AI chatbot:** a Claude-powered assistant with a floating chat UI and a DOSO-specific system prompt.
- **Canva:** social media designs organised in a structured folder system.
- **Figma and FigJam:** design files plus an organisation chart, process flow, sponsor journey and operations calendar.

**Tools:** Canva, Figma, FigJam, Claude
