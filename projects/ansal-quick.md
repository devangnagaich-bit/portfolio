# Ansal Quick

A hyperlocal quick-commerce platform for the Ansal API locality in Lucknow, modelled on Blinkit and Zepto.

## Features

- One merged React application with three role-based views: **Buyer**, **Vendor** and **Delivery Partner**
- Shared storage connecting the three views
- Live order tracking
- Vendor inventory management
- Delivery partner earnings tracking and payouts
- Star ratings and reviews
